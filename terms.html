Terms of Privacy

Transparency, independence, and reader trust guide everything we publish. These terms outline how we handle your data, our editorial principles, and the ground rules for using Duvane Architectural Matter.

Last updated: 1 April 2025 · Version 2.1

Our commitment

Duvane Architectural Matter is an independent journal. We are not owned by any media conglomerate, nor do we accept sponsored content or affiliate commissions. Our only source of revenue is voluntary reader memberships.

This means we can write freely — and we take that responsibility seriously. The following terms describe how we protect your privacy and the principles that govern our editorial work.

Editorial independence

Every article is written or reviewed by practising professionals. We do not publish content to please advertisers or partners. Any potential conflict of interest is disclosed at the top of the relevant article.

We are funded by readers. This allows us to remain free from commercial influence and to publish articles that are genuinely useful — even when they challenge conventional wisdom or popular trends.

Privacy principles

We collect the absolute minimum data required to run our website. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Ever.

  • No third-party trackers — we don’t use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any external advertising trackers.
  • Anonymous analytics — we use a privacy-first, self-hosted analytics tool that does not collect IP addresses or personal identifiers.
  • Newsletter — if you subscribe, we store only your email address and the date you joined. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email.
  • Cookies — we use only essential cookies for basic site functionality (e.g., remembering if you dismissed a notice). No tracking cookies.

Data we collect (and why)

Log data: Like most websites, our servers automatically record basic information when you visit — browser type, pages visited, and time spent. This data is anonymised and used only to understand aggregate usage patterns. We cannot identify you from it.

Contact information: If you email us or subscribe to our newsletter, we retain your email address and correspondence. We never share this with third parties.

Search queries: Search terms entered on our site are not linked to any individual user. We use them only to improve our content.

Comments / contributions: If you submit a guest article, pitch, or comment, we retain the information you provide for editorial purposes.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete your data (unless we are required to keep it for legal reasons).
  • Withdraw consent for newsletter communications at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@duvanearchitectural.com. We respond within 30 days.

Intellectual property

All original content on Duvane Architectural Matter — including articles, images, illustrations, and data — is owned by us or our contributors and is protected by copyright. You may share short excerpts with a link back to the original article, but reproduction in full is not permitted without written permission.

We sometimes use images from third parties under fair use or with permission. If you believe your copyright has been infringed, please contact us immediately.

Terms of use

By using this website, you agree to the following:

  • You will not use the site for any unlawful purpose or in a way that could damage or impair its availability.
  • You will not attempt to gain unauthorised access to any part of the site or its underlying infrastructure.
  • You will not scrape, republish, or redistribute our content without permission.
  • You acknowledge that information on the site is for general informational purposes and does not constitute professional advice.

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the new terms.

Third-party links

Our articles sometimes link to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to read their policies before providing any personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may revise these Terms of Privacy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision. We will notify newsletter subscribers of any significant changes.

Questions?

If you have any questions about these terms, your data, or our editorial policies, please reach out. We’re a small team and we read every email.

editor@duvanearchitectural.com

We typically respond within 5 working days.
