Frequently asked questions

How do I choose a colour palette for a small room? +
Start with light, warm neutrals for walls and floors, then introduce one or two deeper accents in textiles or art. Avoid high-contrast schemes that visually shrink the space.
What’s the ideal ceiling height for residential comfort? +
While 2.4–2.7 m is standard, 2.8–3.0 m significantly improves perceived spaciousness. In renovations, removing dropped ceilings is often the most effective upgrade.
Are open-plan layouts always better? +
Not always. Open plans suit social living but can lack acoustic and visual privacy. Consider zoned layouts with partial walls or furniture dividers for balance.
How much does a full interior renovation cost per square metre? +
Costs vary widely by region and specification. As a rough guide, a mid-range renovation starts around €800–1,500 per m², while high-end custom work can exceed €2,500 per m².
