We believe good design is evidence-based, accessible, and deeply human.

Duvane Architectural Matter is an independent journal for architects, interior designers, and anyone who cares about the built environment. We publish practical, research-backed articles that help you make better spaces.

Our story

Founded in 2021, Duvane Architectural Matter began as a small newsletter shared among a group of practising architects and interior designers. Frustrated by the lack of rigorous, non-commercial design writing, we set out to create a publication that treats design as a serious subject — without losing the joy of it.

Today, we reach over 80,000 readers each month, from homeowners planning a renovation to professionals specifying materials for large-scale projects. Every article is written or reviewed by someone who has hands-on experience in the field.

Editorial independence

We do not accept sponsored content, paid placements, or affiliate commissions. Our recommendations are based solely on research, professional expertise, and a commitment to our readers.

We are funded entirely by our readers through a voluntary membership model. This allows us to remain free from commercial influence and to publish articles that are genuinely useful — even when they challenge conventional wisdom or popular trends.

Corrections policy: We aim for accuracy. If you spot an error, please contact us and we will correct it promptly.

“Architecture is not just about shelter. It is about the quality of the space between walls — the light, the air, the proportions, the materials. These are the things that shape daily life.”
— The Duvane Editorial Charter

What we stand for

Rigour

We ground our articles in research, building science, and professional practice. No myths, no fluff.

Accessibility

Design knowledge shouldn’t be locked behind jargon. We write clearly for a broad, intelligent audience.

Independence

Reader-supported, ad-free, and free from commercial influence. Our only loyalty is to our readers.
